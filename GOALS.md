# Gamma Goals

## Mission & Thesis

Gamma is an empirical workbench for model evaluation, distillation, capability
transfer, backend comparison and algorithmic composition research. Model and
program behavior must be supported by reproducible evidence, not inferred from
an attractive interface or a compact encoding. Research ambitions and measured
capabilities remain separate.

## Intended Beneficiaries

Researchers compare models, programs and reusable languages under explicit
contracts. People explore binary code composition through BITS. Autonomous
systems consume independently qualified capabilities. Gamma supplies research
methods and evidence; neighboring projects retain production runtime, compiler,
editor and orchestration authority.

## Desired Outcomes

The [versioned goal contract](projects/algorithm_solver/contracts/algorithm_solver_goals_v2.json)
defines these five families and aliases historical goal IDs without rewriting
old results. JavaScript and Python qualify independently.

1. **Recognition.** **1a** retrieves separately qualified implementations for a
   known specification. **1b** answers one informal description or abstains,
   with zero clarification questions. **1c** expands qualified coverage and
   improves complete retrieval, verification and serving latency.
2. **Reusable languages and joint optimization.** **2a** develops versioned
   64/128/256-entry exact-string vocabularies, with separate compression-first
   and human-readable policies. **2b** jointly optimizes correct algorithms,
   source and dictionaries; successors must satisfy the problem contract, not
   reproduce original source bytes. **2c** studies macros, typed AST constructors
   and editor actions as separate, fully charged representations.
3. **Human BITS fluency and independent solving.** **3a** measures learning and
   retention of stable mappings. **3b** measures solving unfamiliar problems
   without a guide. **3c** compares complete interaction time, inputs, errors and
   corrections with ordinary coding. Playback and interface tests do not prove
   human fluency or independent solving.
4. **Autonomous composition.** **4a** assembles known-problem solutions, reporting
   macro and literal tracks separately. **4b** freezes vocabulary and procedure
   before reserved unseen variants/families. **4c** explores neural primitives
   under the same correctness, cost and evaluation gates. No human steering or
   query-time autoregressive source generation counts as these achievements.
5. **Publication-quality research.** **5a** seeks a defensible novel mechanism
   under existing M6 review gates. **5b** seeks matched literature-leading results
   under existing M5 gates. Neither a proposed target nor internal readiness
   guarantees novelty, venue acceptance or permission to submit.

## Current owner priority (2026-10-25)

Goals 1a/1b/1c and a complete consistently represented JavaScript/Python catalogue
now lead the [prospective roadmap](projects/algorithm_solver/catalogue/ROADMAP-v1.md).
Its eligible universe is independent of current source availability. The literal
frontier additionally investigates 16/32-entry (4/5-bit) profiles alongside 64/128/256;
partial coverage remains explicit. Historical M1–M6 gates/results are preserved,
and original compact-library M1 remains unmet. This priority change does not
reclassify protected populations or qualify an existing result retroactively.

## Operating Loops

Freeze contracts and budgets; execute bounded experiments; independently verify
outputs and costs; preserve failures; rebuild reports; choose the next hypothesis.
Joint composition alternates qualified source alternatives and vocabulary search.
Keep execution separate from reporting. Existing model evaluation, distillation
and SAME-R workflows retain their named contracts and evidence requirements.

## Strategic Constraints

Preserve original sources, runtime/test hashes, licenses, protected populations,
legacy goal IDs and all [M1–M6 thresholds](projects/algorithm_solver/literals/MILESTONES.md).
Exact literal decoding reconstructs each selected successor. Qualify behavior,
mutation/alias, numeric/Unicode and complexity against the pinned problem
contract; finite tests are evidence, not proof. Count helpers, operands, framing,
decoders, models and interaction controls. Keep recognition, representation,
human solving and autonomous solving independently measured.

The [experiment index](projects/algorithm_solver/literals/INDEX.md),
[research protocol](projects/algorithm_solver/literals/RESEARCH.md) and existing
status documents hold changing results. Follow [INTENT](INTENT.md) and
[component charters](CATSCAN.md). The
[enwiki9 project](projects/enwiki9/operations/runtime/project_status.json) remains
retired by owner decision on 2026-10-08; preserve its archive and require explicit
owner reopening before research or execution.

## Explicit Exclusions

No hidden answer lookup presented as composition, uncounted source channels,
unverified capability claims, or automatic publication. Gamma does not own
production inference, compiler correctness, editor hosting or multi-tenant cloud
services. Goal organization alone authorizes no new training, downloads,
protected scoring, deployments or external submissions.
