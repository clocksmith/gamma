# Personal infrastructure and no-writing implementation

Date: 2026-09-08. Executable 0.20.0; physical rules 0.12.0-rc.1-test;
engine 0.23.0. This is implementation evidence, not balance or human-play evidence.

## Rules implemented

Mega-Cluster II, Fusion III, and Quantum IV are personal hosted upgrades, once
per institution, with no mandatory chain. They share Build, attachment,
relocation, and flipped-chip completion. Fusion is neither unique nor an ordinary
Generator and uses no Generator slot. Mega-Cluster needs one connected host;
Quantum produces Capability instead of an immediate award.

Provisional common price: 3 Runway, 1 Compute, 1 Scrutiny. Connected-host yields:
Mega-Cluster +2 Compute; Quantum +1 Capability. Fusion powers its own host and
same/adjacent owned Facilities. No immediate project Mandate or Capability.
These amounts require playtesting; the earlier construction study does not
validate this replacement.

Trust uses a monotonic highest-awarded milestone. Historical Era objectives
use one numbered cube track; other objectives read current state. AGI has
No/Recognized spaces. Printed starting Trust and final state determine the
World Ending. Joint Venture Era orientation is specified without handwriting.

rival/stale host rejection before payment, forged-payload canonicalization,

threshold awards, and nonstarting Fusion autonomy after Generator removal.

Capability from Compute totals. Price and yield prose resolve numeric sources.

releases and study records remain intact.
