# Capacity Operator counterplay results — 2026-10-11

Component: Mandate 2038 policy evidence. Intent preserved. No rules, default-policy, version, publication or deployment change.

**Result: no qualified counter or adaptive recovery; no economy correction selected.** Stronger opponents narrowed Capacity Operator’s observed lead, especially in greedy play. The targeted greedy responder improved its own result, but its gain and Capacity suppression do not clear the frozen simultaneous intervals. Weighted response and adaptation selection retained their parents. These outcomes do not establish an unbeatable strategy, permanent balance, or human enjoyment.

## Decision, limits and surface audit

Keep the current economy. This bounded search does not identify a persistent advantage that equally prepared rivals cannot counter, and it does not establish robust counterplay. Equal backend and search budget control preparation, not proven equal skill. The mutation family only changes existing action, decision and negotiation weights; it does not discover arbitrary new conditional plans, optimized denial, long-horizon negotiation or human adaptation. Search selection is noisy; guard intervals are broad; no experienced humans or independently controlled LLMs were tested.

For human testing, use the retained greedy matchup as a concrete question: can players maintain Capability and equipment while choosing useful deployment windows, and can Capacity adapt without becoming the only viable route? Retain the weighted null and the canonical balance failures. Do not automatically weaken Research, change production or promote the selected policies. Any later economy proposal needs a named causal mechanism, a fresh one-lever comparison and explicit selection.
