## Decision

1. Reject scalar `imperialLateCapabilityThresholdMandate: 1` as a universal
   physical rule.
2. Preserve the late-public-validation mechanism as the first Demis lever
   with a strong, causally demonstrated standings effect.
3. Test one refinement: Capability 9 and 12 score one Mandate for Demis, but
   Capability 12 returns to the canonical two Mandate when four rivals are
   present. This models broad independent validation and restores roughly
   half a point per five-player appearance.
