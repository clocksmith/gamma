## Selected next probe

> Each of your first three Customers scores 2 Mandate. Your fourth and fifth
> Customers score 1 Mandate each.

This preserves:

- every Customer;
- every Customer's Runway production;
- every Capability requirement and Deploy cost;
- Mark's starting Customer and distribution advantage;
- all spatial and negotiation rules.

It changes only the public Mandate conversion at market saturation, the exact
source identified by the attribution. The maximum correction is two Mandate;
an Adoption winner at four Customers loses one, close to the measured score
gap. It is ineligible for physical promotion unless a fresh one-lever matrix
improves winner-path diversity without breaking faction parity, making Deploy
noncompetitive, or creating a new dominant route.
