**Status:** historical evidence provenance. Current rules are in
[`../rules.md`](../rules.md) and [`../components/game.json`](../components/game.json).

The candidate's paired comparison is retained at
[`data/single-generator-default.rules-configurations.json`](data/single-generator-default.rules-configurations.json).

play option and does not qualify the current game's balance or teachability.

contention, and the retained Production snapshot. Human playtests still need to
establish teachability, negotiation quality, agency, and the usefulness of
