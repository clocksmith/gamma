Allocation sales mostly saturate by four players. New Architecture licensing,
Shovels opportunities, and the number of rivals receiving the Round IV grant

supplier identity and remain distinct from the five-player Mandate step.

Foundry's established three- and four-player strength exists while Everybody
Gets a GPU awards no Mandate. Removing the five-player point therefore cannot
be the general correction. It also leaves the Round IV program asking Foundry
to accelerate every rival in exchange only for Scrutiny removal, weakening
the public-value side of the ability without relocating victory conversion to
real customer demand.

## Decision

1. Reject `foundryGpuMandateEnabled: false` as a standalone physical change.
2. Retain one Mandate per four rivals as the canonical hypothesis while the
   broader Foundry conversion package is diagnosed.
3. Preserve starting Compute and the supplier abilities. The next eligible
   hypothesis must move victory conversion toward realized rival demand rather
   than make Jensen compute-poor.
4. Do not combine a Foundry candidate with the promotion-eligible Elon
   candidate until each isolated effect is understood.
5. Add configuration-specific faction standing summaries before the next
   fresh canonical three/four/five-player audit.
