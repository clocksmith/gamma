`lane-margin-v1` with a one-point hybrid margin. A winner is classified as a
hybrid when the top two scoring-lane signals finish within one action,
Customer, Facility, or comparable end-state unit. Lane names use canonical
ordering, so the same pair cannot fragment into two labels.

found that `56.70%` of five-player package winner credit had its top two lanes
