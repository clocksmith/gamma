## Demis calibration: remove one saved Capability

Across `2,670` paired Demis appearances:

- win-share delta: `-3.596` percentage points;
- mean-score delta: `-0.464`;
- bounded multiplicity-adjusted interval: `[-10.001, +2.810]` percentage
  points.

Partially pooled Demis win rates changed:

| Players | Canonical | Candidate | Delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | 50.53% | 45.70% | -4.83 pp |
| 4 | 36.80% | 33.35% | -3.45 pp |
| 5 | 24.76% | 22.24% | -2.52 pp |

Canonical Scientific Method used `1,593` times and preserved `3,969`
Capability. The candidate used it `1,603` times and removed `1,603`
Capability, or approximately `0.600` Capability per Demis appearance.

This lever is rejected. It leaves Demis above four-player parity, its bounded
paired interval crosses zero, and—more importantly—it violates faction truth
by making a successful scientific rescue produce a less capable model. It is
calibration only: removing about `0.600` Capability per appearance moved
Demis's score by about `0.464` Mandate.

The next Demis probe must preserve Capability and Research reliability while
testing the registered bottleneck of public validation. The selected
theme-valid candidate withholds at most one Capability-threshold Mandate when
Scientific Method saves the run and that bank crosses a threshold.

fresh-seed confirmation, not promoted to the physical game.
