## Decision

Select none of the four candidates. Do not weaken faction identity or add
starting-resource inflation to chase deterministic policies that rarely use
the relevant mechanic.

The next validity step is strategy adaptation, not another unregistered
physical-rule change. Evolve the weakest authored personas against the current
rules, then rerun the unified matrix with their inspectable champions as
diagnostic opponents. This tests whether the practical Capability and faction
spreads reflect real strategic inevitability or an underdeveloped policy
ecology. Physical rules remain frozen until that counterplay check is complete.
