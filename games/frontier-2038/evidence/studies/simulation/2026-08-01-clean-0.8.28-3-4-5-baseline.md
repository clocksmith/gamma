## Three-player interpretation

The earlier 0.8.27 handoff's three-player Safety Laboratory lead did not
reproduce. Mirevanta Works led this fresh 0.8.28 seed bank, while Safety
Laboratory finished between Mirevanta and Coalition. The observed range also
moved inside the provisional 15-point screen.

Coalition Lab nevertheless finished lowest at every supported count. At three
players, the authored-policy cells also changed direction and magnitude:
Mirevanta Works reached 52.27% with Capability Rusher and 45.08% with Power
Broker, while Safety Laboratory ranged from 20.16% with Balanced Operator to
44.92% with Infrastructure Compounder. Seat zero won 36.91% compared with
31.78% and 31.31% for seats one and two, still inside the seat-range gate.

The correct hypothesis is therefore a faction by Initiative seat by authored
deterministic-policy interaction. It is not evidence of an intrinsic Safety
Laboratory, Mirevanta Works, or Coalition Lab rules advantage. A common-seed
paired isolation must hold the deterministic backend fixed and rotate faction,
seat, and focal authored policy separately.
