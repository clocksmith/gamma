## Interpretation

The matrix demonstrates that the seven-factor runner, backend rotation,
defection-capable negotiation, causal supplier attribution, partial pooling,
adaptive allocation, and promotion gate operate together from a clean source.
It does not establish the registered precision target. Therefore:

- no faction, strategy, backend, matchup, or rule value is declared balanced;
- the absence of a dominance flag is not evidence of absence;
- the rare declaration confirms a legal path, not a stable human frequency;
- supplier top-half rate is descriptive and not proof that human bargains feel
  fair; and
- the high forced-no-op count requires replay and physical-test interpretation
  before it can motivate a rules change.
