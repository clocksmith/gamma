# M3T4 2038: Optional Tactic Rules

**Rules version:** 0.5.0-rc.34-test

**Baseline status:** Deferred; do not use for baseline play or balance evidence

**Rules companion:** [How to Play and Rules Reference](./core-rules.md)

This document preserves the complete optional Tactic module without
interrupting the baseline teaching sequence. Tactics and secret objectives are
not used in the baseline game.

## Deck and setup

Use 3 copies of each of the twelve Tactics, for a
thirty-six-card deck. Deal one Tactic to each player during setup. Draw one at
the beginning of every round, including Round I. Keep at most three and discard
excess cards after drawing. Maximum normal five-player demand is twenty-five
cards.

## Timing

Players may play at most one Tactic per cycle. Unless its card states another
window, play a Tactic during your own resolution. A Tactic occupies the
optional modifier slot and never grants an additional Action.

Open Letter is the module’s only off-turn timing window. Play it after a
Government vote’s options are announced but before any votes are committed.
The module has no off-turn cancellations.

## Card contracts

- **Cloud Partnership:** Pay 1 Runway for 2 Compute; another player gains 1 Runway.
- **API Price Cut:** Deploy for zero Compute; that Customer produces no Runway this round.
- **Open Letter:** After a Government vote’s options are announced but before any votes are committed, choose one option and add one public vote to it.
- **Model Card:** Remove 1 Scrutiny after Deploy.
- **Talent Raid:** Recruit a neutral Expert for 1 Runway.
- **Board Reshuffle:** Ready Organize or Influence.
- **Weights Leak:** Immediately resolve one powered rival Facility’s printed production as if it were yours.
- **Emergency Pause:** End a failed Training Run with no Capability and no Scrutiny.
- **Custom Silicon:** One Facility permanently produces +1 Compute.
- **Government Contract:** If Trust is at least four, gain 2 Runway.
- **Benchmark Optimization:** Gain +1 Capability after successful Research and add 1 Scrutiny.
- **Interconnection Waiver:** Gain 1 Build discount and 1 Trust.

Discard a Tactic after it resolves. The Tactic discard pile is not reshuffled.
