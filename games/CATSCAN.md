# CATSCAN: Gamma games

Parent: [Gamma](../CATSCAN.md)

## Target

Host playable systems whose authored rules, runtime behavior, and evidence remain internally coherent and distinct from model benchmarks.

## Authority

- Owns separation and navigation of game projects under `games/`.
- Does not own a child game's rules, balance claims, or publication decisions.

## Scope

- Applies to authored game systems, runtimes, simulations, and play evidence under `games/`.

## Contracts

- Input: Repository-wide evidence and authority discipline from the [Gamma charter](../CATSCAN.md).
- Output: Authored game systems, runtimes, simulations, and play evidence.

## Invariants

- Authored rules and generated projections remain distinguishable.
- Simulations are never presented as human playtests.
- Child games own their own canonical mechanics and promotion boundaries.

## Acceptance

- Each maintained game exposes its source authority and executable validation path.
- Evidence: [Mandate 2038 package contract](2038/package.json).

## Non-goals

- Imposing one rules engine, theme, or balance policy on every game.

## Freedom

Any mechanism is permitted if it preserves these boundaries and passes the acceptance evidence.
