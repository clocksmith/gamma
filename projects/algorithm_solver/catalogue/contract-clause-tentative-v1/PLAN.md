# ONE prospective tentative compatibility signal

Synthetic-only reusable ranking calculation, not a model campaign or acceptance policy. No expected IDs, cohort lookup, source availability, model or service inputs. The consumed confirmation is excluded from design/examples. Existing policies/calibration claims remain unchanged.

For candidate `i`, role `r`, take the maximum **raw cosine in [-1,1]** among supplied query-span × authoritative-clause pairs with that role. Average observed-role maxima in stable sorted order with `math.fsum`. Let `T_i = observed_role_mean - original_raw_cosine_i`. No authoritative clauses or no observed pairs means `T_i=0` and UNKNOWN, not failure. The ONE declared adjusted ranking score is `existing_hybrid_score_i + 0.05*T_i`. Weight is fixed, not swept. Raw, existing-hybrid and new-adjusted rankings remain separate. Ties retain the pinned original universe order; permutations of observation/evidence/clauses do not change results.

Bounds: raw cosine [-1,1], input existing-hybrid [-1.05,1.05], T [-2,2], additive adjustment [-0.1,0.1], new score [-1.15,1.15] (tiny arithmetic endpoint tolerance only). The new score is uncalibrated. It does NOT consume the adapter's affine 0..1 compatibility values; exact `score_units=raw_cosine_-1_to_1`, encoder/prompt SHA and embedding dimension bindings must equal operator expectations. Binding assertions are not self-authenticating: a future integration must verify actual vector/runtime provenance before invoking this pure function. A normalized value falsely labeled cosine cannot be detected from numbers alone.

All 4,081 competitors remain. Title-only rivals get no authority adjustment, not elimination.
