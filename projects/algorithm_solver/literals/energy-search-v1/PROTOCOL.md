# Energy-guided candidate search: bounded interface v1

This local, private experiment belongs to goal v2:4c and search guidance in v2:2b. It preserves the current M1 gates. It creates no model, training run, candidate admission, service, public artifact or protected evaluation.

Authority and tracking remain in [Gamma GOALS.md](../../../../GOALS.md), the [root CATSCAN](../../../../CATSCAN.md) → [projects CATSCAN](../../../CATSCAN.md) → [algorithm-solver CATSCAN](../../CATSCAN.md) chain, and the existing [research loop](../RESEARCH_LOOP.md). This local protocol is one bounded experiment beneath those records; it does not replace them or change [M1–M6](../MILESTONES.md).

The three input objects are distinct: an exact literal candidate piece, a partial assembly with an explicit prefix and remaining holes, and a complete vocabulary plus one qualified source choice per problem. Partial assemblies are never scored as solved programs. Source variants can be algorithmically different; the trusted qualification manifest must bind each to the same problem/domain/effect/numeric/complexity contract.

Use the existing pinned `../joint-v1/reachable_core.py` shortest-path parser and `../readable-v1/literal_codec.py` LTD1/LTV1 codec. All emitted source, helpers and imports must already be in the selected source string and reconstruct exactly. Operands, control bits and framing are charged explicitly; no raw-source escape, source substitution or hidden helper injection is accepted. The flat-literal harness does not implement macros or a structured editor.

Eligibility is checked before either scorer is called. Source, contract and receipt hashes must match an explicitly reviewed qualification manifest. Required output, mutation, alias, numeric and complexity gates must pass; finite tests remain evidence, not proof. A low energy cannot override eligibility. The first runnable fixture is synthetic and unexecuted, and therefore must carry `synthetic_fixture_only`; it cannot be reported as qualified LeetCode output. Real-data execution requires a coordinator-reviewed envelope linking existing receipts. The harness verifies that envelope and its immutable proof files, but is not an independent semantic judge.

Compare the same frozen proposals, source pool, track, width and split using the same exact-cost evaluations and scorer-call caps. The deterministic baseline uses exact package bits, then input bits, then stable identity. The learned-energy interface receives only declared features, not the exact target label. The first comparison is an offline ranking/replay harness: it does not establish search acceleration because the complete proposal costs are evaluated for both arms. Record model bytes, scorer time, exact-cost time, ranking regret, rejected states and budgets. Model identity, training-source hashes and split must be supplied before evaluating a learned scorer; no model is supplied here.

Candidate proposals include deterministic add/delete/swap and source-alternative rewrite moves. Candidate generation distinguishes human-specified complete pieces from recurrence-mined strings and preserves their provenance. The pinned portable human-seed fixture retains exact general snippets and historical hashes from the count-alignment witnesses (#560 and #781), with their unqualified successor status at fixture capture explicit. The original private run verified their occurrence; this portable adapter does not carry or reread the full private programs. Do not rank that source pool as qualified until independent qualification receipts are bound.

Splits are frozen by family, with source-hash and problem/alias checks. Previously exposed development remains exposed even when divided into train/validation/test partitions. No unseen-generalization claim follows from such a partition. For the initial synthetic fixture, all splits are mechanism fixtures only.

Bounds: new lane ≤4 MiB; one CPU13; synthetic tests/smoke ≤20 CPU seconds and ≤512 MiB. No downloads, dependencies, GPU, training or canonical edits. Parent coordinates the CPU test window with active indexing. Preserve failures and compare readable and compression policies separately. A later real-data benchmark must freeze qualification envelopes, genuine held-out boundaries, candidate policies, compute and model costs before any training or evaluation.

## Two separate tracks

**Track A: development optimization.** Optimize a development vocabulary and choices among separately qualified implementations. Repeated inspection, rewriting, mining, failure repair and rescoring are development exposure. This harness currently implements the state, eligibility, discrete-neighborhood, exact-cost and offline-ranking mechanics for this track. Its smoke data is synthetic only. Names such as train/validation/test do not make exposed data unseen.

**Track B: description to assembly.** Freeze vocabulary, mappings, model, candidate-generation/search procedure, stopping policy and compute budget before receiving independently reserved descriptions. Score first-attempt assembly, abstention, costs and full correctness independently. The partial-assembly schema is preparation for this track; no description parser, autonomous assembly procedure or Track B evaluation is implemented here. Do not transfer Track A reconstruction scores into this track. Near-duplicate and family exclusions must be checked by the coordinator before a one-time confirmation evaluation; any feedback after inspection returns the material to development.

## Repeatable evidence loop and promotion

1. Record source, problem/family/alias, contract, receipt, candidate and run identities. Diagnose cost, coverage and failure clusters against the previous immutable baseline.
2. State a falsifiable hypothesis. Predeclare designed-only, mined-only and combined seeds; never hide failed denominators. The smoke includes all three. Designed-only uses exactly the concrete human seeds, with no free alphabet or uncounted framing strings. If none of its states can reconstruct the two selected synthetic programs, report zero coverage out of two, null best/cost and all rejection reasons. It must not crash or convert missing source into a finite cost.
3. Freeze split/exclusions, source alternatives, candidate pool and each proposed add/delete/swap/rewrite move before scoring. Preserve exact seed strings and full adapters/helpers. Distinguish complete readable units from partial lines or arbitrary compression fragments.
4. Independently qualify new source alternatives before they become eligible. Retain invalid, unqualified, unreachable and high-cost proposals as informative negative records, but never treat a low predicted energy as qualification. Original failures remain immutable.
5. Compare equal-budget non-neural and neural searches only after both procedures exist. Count proposal generation, exact evaluations, scorer calls, CPU/GPU time, training cost, model storage and decoder/package costs. The present offline-ranking comparison shares all exact evaluations and therefore tests ranking mechanics only.
6. Audit reconstruction, numerical/mutation/complexity contracts, independent receipts and cost accounting. Retain non-dominated coverage/input/storage/readability tradeoffs and regressions. Readability proxies are not human memorability measurements.
7. Promote a research candidate only after a matched bounded confirmation reproduces its gain without eligibility regressions, an independent reviewer verifies the claims and complete costs, and the intended publication/UI scope is authorized. A failed hypothesis is recorded and informs the next bounded development experiment. No automatic live promotion occurs here.

## Trainable energy specification still required

No trainable energy implementation or trained checkpoint is supplied. A future proposal must declare its input representation separately for pieces, partial assemblies and whole-library/source-choice states; exact cost target or pairwise-ranking objective; qualification-filtered labels; positive/negative sampling; near-duplicate/family split; initialization seed; model/checkpoint identity; training/scoring compute; and negative-selection policy. Useful negatives include reachable but expensive states and contract-incompatible, unqualified or unreachable proposals. Invalid examples may train a separate eligibility predictor, but the actual hard eligibility checks still run independently and cannot be replaced by that predictor. Negative mining from validation/test feedback is leakage and requires a new development version, not a relabeled confirmation result.

The scorer interface records version, artifact hash, seed, model bytes and training-manifest identity. A handmade or constant score is a baseline/interface test, never a trained EBM. Potential energy-guided search benefit, neural generalization and superiority over established methods remain unmeasured.

The tiny synthetic reference predeclares three human seed additions over the observed-character control and at most two alternatives for each of two programs (at most 32 states). It records a piece support/occurrence matrix, exact marginal package savings, textual overlap and pair complementarity, plus successful/failed state traces. A fixed-source greedy policy is compared with its matched finite exhaustive dictionary optimum; source-choice opportunity is a separate metric. This diagnoses local heuristic gaps only. General candidate dependency analysis, broad rewrite generation, learned scoring, unseen description-to-assembly, and real-data qualification integration remain absent.

## Concrete next scorer specification — proposed, not trained

The first trainable comparison would operate only on complete Track A vocabulary/source-choice states. Candidate-piece and partial-assembly scorers require separately declared representations and are not covered by this first objective.

Use the existing fixed seven-feature schema, in this order: `entries`, `dictionary_utf8_bytes`, `source_bytes`, `multi_problem_occurrences`, `width`, `problems`, `character_entries`. Each is a nonnegative count produced by `features`; `multi_problem_occurrences` is the number of selected strings occurring in more than one distinct selected problem, not the number of source variants. No exact package cost, test verdict, problem ID, source hash or target rank is an input feature. Any normalization statistics must be fit only on training states and frozen with the scorer artifact. A small linear energy is the first trainable baseline; it is a pairwise ranking model, not an implementation of Energy-Based Transformers or evidence of general reasoning.

For each pair, states must have the identical target problem set, track, ID width and full coverage, and both must pass all qualification gates. Let `a` have lower complete package cost than `b`, using exact dictionary storage plus all padded packets; decoder/model costs are separately charged and are constant within a matched scorer comparison. Prefer `E(a) < E(b)` with **pairwise logistic ranking loss** `log(1 + exp(E(a) - E(b)))`. Tied-cost states contribute no preference pair. Rank by complete cost first; selection-only improvements are recorded separately and cannot silently change the objective.

Freeze family/near-duplicate-separated training, validation and confirmation-test manifests before producing labels. A pair compares states of the same development problem or identical development micro-corpus; no pair crosses a split. Preserve all source/contract/receipt hashes, seed provenance, candidate/move IDs and source-choice identities. The existing scorer interface records the training manifest, model/scorer version, initialization seed, artifact hash and model bytes. Never train on feedback from validation or one-time confirmation results.

The initial negative-sampling proposal is bounded: at most 64 predeclared add/delete/swap/rewrite proposals per training state, and at most four eligible negatives per lower-cost state. Begin with deterministic uniform selection by frozen seed; after fitting on training data only, one declared hard-negative round may choose eligible higher-cost states that the current model incorrectly ranks below the lower-cost state. Preserve both the initial and selected pools and count every exact evaluation/scorer call. Contract-incompatible, failed, unqualified and unreachable states stay in an eligibility-diagnostics set; they never become positive qualified examples, and this ranking loss does not bypass their hard rejection. No candidate source is executed by the scorer harness.

Before any training launch, freeze the optimizer, step/parameter limit, normalization, seed, pair/mining budget and unchanged resource envelope in a successor experiment identity. Required evaluation artifacts are: train/validation/test provenance manifests; pair/negative IDs and exact labels; model/normalization artifact; complete compute/storage receipt; same-proposal exact-cost baseline; equal-budget guided-search comparison if such a search is implemented; ranking regret, eligibility failures, coverage/cost Pareto results and independent validation. The current offline runner is not that guided-search experiment. Promotion requires reproducible benefit beyond the matched non-neural control without lower coverage, weaker contracts, uncounted costs or validation leakage.

## Completed mechanism evidence

The original private run on 2026-10-21 at 22:36:29 UTC passed **24 software tests**, completed **18 synthetic arms**, and enumerated the **32-state reference** in 0.160 CPU seconds with 28,585,984 bytes peak RSS. It remains unchanged. Designed-only controls retained zero out of two coverage and null best/cost; the tiny greedy dictionary gap and source-choice opportunity were both zero. The portable rerun and its aggregate receipt are documented in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). No model was supplied or trained, no candidate program was executed, and no real problem was qualified. These results establish harness mechanics, not EBM quality, search acceleration, generalization or milestone progress.
