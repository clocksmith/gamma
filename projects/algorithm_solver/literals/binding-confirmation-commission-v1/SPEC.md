## Population and independent authorship

Commission tests for **all 400 existing development IDs**, preserving their
300-fit/100-validation labels. Do not sample by observed naming changes, action
gain, failure history, difficulty or author convenience. Repeated cases do not add
problems to coverage. JavaScript is the only language qualified by this commission.

Assign an author and a different reviewer who did not build or tune item 13.
Neither Gamma candidate developer nor a fork carrying developer history may fill
those roles. Record identities, permitted inputs, actual exposure, tool use and
conflicts. Agent-authored/reviewed tests remain agent evidence; unknown foundation
model exposure must be disclosed. Agreement between developers is not independence.

Provide the author an approved allowlist of original problem contracts: statement,
interface, input domain, output equivalence, mutation/alias semantics, numeric and
Unicode rules, and complexity requirements. Include only the permitted 400 IDs.
Do not provide selected/original implementations, naming traces, dictionaries,
packets, development cases, per-problem costs or candidate outcomes. The reviewer
may inspect those same requirements and authored tests/oracles, never candidate
outputs or source code. A custodian can use hashes/metadata to enforce the separation.

For each problem commission at least 16 distinct private cases, unless the
reviewer certifies that the valid domain has fewer than 16 distinct inputs and
exhausts it. Allocate cases across normal behavior, boundary/degenerate inputs,
answer-changing distinctions and applicable numeric/effect constraints. Record an
applicability matrix rather than forcing invalid cases into an interface. Include
an independently derived executable oracle or a justified property checker;
metamorphic relations alone must not label an arbitrary answer correct. Generate
expected observations outside the candidate sandbox. Mutation/alias observations
and allowed nondeterministic outputs must follow the original contract.

The reviewer checks domain validity, distinctness, oracle independence, expected
results and coverage without consulting a candidate. Retain every initial packet,
revision, rejection and reason. Resolve defects before sealing; insufficient
independent coverage for any ID blocks a full 400-program confirmation, rather
than dropping it from the denominator. No requirement or resource-limit changes.

## Pass rule fixed before authorship

Report two booleans separately; neither is M1 qualification.

**Independent correctness confirmation** passes only with all 400 selected
candidate programs independently decoded to their sealed hashes and passing every
reviewed private check; every original contract/qualification receipt remains
valid; every ID meets the authoring coverage rule; no missing evidence,
infrastructure error, source drift or budget violation occurs; and the candidate
contains at most 256 literal entries with no whole-solution/body entries. Score
matched controls independently and retain any control failure. A control failure
prevents a clean comparative confirmation until separately resolved; it must not
silently excuse a candidate failure.

**Recorded development advantage reproduced** passes only if frozen packet costs
exactly reproduce the existing 100-validation comparison and its unchanged
readiness predicates: all 100 decode/qualify; clean audits; at least 5% fewer total
actions; strictly lower median and p90 actions; at least 60 improved and at most
25 worsened programs; nonincreasing single-character selection-bit fraction; raw
package at most 105% of the matched control. Recompute total package, framing,
padding, dictionary and decoder costs. One Finish per program remains an action.
This is reproducibility of consumed-development costs, not fresh compactness evidence.

The expected existing values are 8,504 versus 9,048 actions, 75/21/4
better/same/worse, 61.5/199 median/p95 **selections**, 49.89% single-character
selection bits and 14,724 package bytes. Report selections and actions separately.

**M1 stays unchanged:** 400/500 frozen problems, at most 256 shared literal
entries, median at most 32 and p95 at most 96 selections, at most 10% of selection
bits from single-character entries, zero whole-solution entries. The existing
candidate misses the cost gates. New tests cannot change that fact, substitute a
400-program denominator for 500, or establish M2/human fluency. See
[MILESTONES.md](../MILESTONES.md).

## Proposed second stage: fresh-program dictionary transfer

This is a concrete additional commission for the owner to approve, not permission
for the team to author or open new material. It addresses fresh compactness
transfer separately from the first stage's correctness confirmation.

Commission **exactly 100 newly written JavaScript programs for 100 distinct
independently selected problem contracts**. A custodian first seals the problem
manifest and family/difficulty strata, before implementation authoring and before
any candidate cost is measured. Require an exclusion audit against all previously
used development programs, final held-out 100, protected 66 and their aliases;
none of those reservations is implicitly opened. Selection cannot depend on
source length, dictionary coverage or measured gains. The population defines the
scope of the eventual claim; it is not a representative sample merely because
its authors are independent.

Authors and reviewers have the same independence and exposure restrictions as
stage one. They receive requirements only, not candidate/control sources,
dictionaries, naming policies, packets or measured costs. Authors write one
implementation and at least 16 independent cases per problem (or a reviewed
exhaustive smaller domain); an independent reviewer qualifies all implementations
against the sealed contracts before dictionary evaluation. The custodian seals
sources, cases, expected observations and all revision history together. No style
hint is supplied to make the code fit either dictionary. Unencodable sources and
resource failures remain failures in the 100-program denominator.

After a separately approved scored opening, encode each identical qualified
source once with the unchanged item13 dictionary/encoder and once with its pinned
R control dictionary/encoder. No source rewrite, refit, repair, per-program
codebook selection or alternative source choice is allowed. Independently decode
all packets and reproduce their source hashes. Use the same complete charges and
sandbox resource limits as stage one. Report this as **dictionary transfer on
independently written sources**: it cannot by itself replicate the original 6%
benefit of jointly changing source names and dictionary. A fresh evaluation of
that full rewriting pipeline would need its own sealed source-transformation
procedure and matched source controls; do not silently add it to this stage.

Freeze the transfer pass rule now: all 100 programs qualified and decoded under
both arms; at most 256 entries and clean whole-source/body audits; candidate
validation actions at least 5% lower; strictly lower median and p90 actions;
at least 60 improved and at most 25 worsened; nonincreasing single-character
selection-bit fraction; raw package at most 105% of control. Report all predicates,
selection median/p95, absolute character counts, total bytes and paired outcomes,
including failures. This is a fixed 100-program transfer test, not a rescaled
M1 qualification. Stage-one correctness, reproduced development advantage and
stage-two transfer each have separate verdicts; none substitutes for another.
