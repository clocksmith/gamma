## Conditional attribution

Prefix retains and uses zero P entries. Template and union each retain one I
entry, used by one validation program. That program uses **7 fewer actions**
than the searched literal control, and 6 fewer than untouched D11. Its C/N is
40.5263%, versus 40.6091% for literal. The template emits 21 generated bytes
using one root and three paid identifier arguments. Globally, template versus
literal has **1 better, 82 same, 17 worse**: slot replacement costs elsewhere
outweigh this observed local gain. Union ties template exactly.

Common pool eligibility covers 99 programs; template eligibility covers 95.
Eligibility is not actual use. The six predeclared subsets retain full dictionary
and decoder costs and overlap; they cannot be added or used to promote an arm.
On the single I-used program, raw package is 4,255 bytes, **1,379 more** than
literal despite its seven-action saving. Decoder-inclusive difference is
240,540 bytes. All exact IDs, paired costs and empty-subset null ratios remain
in [evidence/results.json](evidence/results.json).

This is evidence of sparse use under this bounded search and grammar. It does
not establish that prefix or template coding is ineffective in general. The
half-source generated-byte guard, paid operands, limited fit pools and search
budget define the tested mechanism. The local template saving remains visible.

## Gates and execution

Each structured arm fails the required 5% action gain, strict median/p90 gain,
60-program breadth and raw-package checks against searched literal. All also
fail character-fraction and raw-package incrementals against D11. Coverage,
qualification, capacity and exact whole-body bans pass. Predictions S1 and
S5–S7 hold; S2–S4 fail. No policy, source or cohort was substituted afterward.

The supervisor completed in **304.73 seconds**, execution 304.59 seconds, on
cores 0–7. Literal stopped at a bounded local optimum after 2,221 trials;
prefix/template/union hit their 300-second clocks after 2,557/2,467/2,572
trials. Rejected coverage trials were 9/9/6/6. These are search outcomes, not
global optimum certificates. All fit seals preceded validation source access.
Qualification reuses item30's exact source receipts; **zero fresh case
executions**. Item23's later unchanged-source exit137 remains separate evidence.
