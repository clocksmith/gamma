# Structured stacking v1 — DRAFT, unbound and not authorized to launch

This is a new, separate 2c experiment. It does not amend item30's flat
[freeze](../stacking-v1/FREEZE.md), any historical record, or M1's literal
representation. Root must obtain Claude review, bind the completed D11 receipts,
resolve review findings, and push the final contract/freeze before execution.
`contract.pending.json` deliberately has `source_bound=false`; the runner requires
a later `contract.json`. No D11 private sources were inspected to write this draft.

## Question and fixed cohort

Can item5 expression-prefix coding and item20 identifier templates yield an
incremental gain together on the predetermined item30 **D11** source/dictionary
output? D11 is fixed by design, not selected using item30 validation outcomes.
Even if another item30 arm wins, do not substitute it. If D11 fails full coverage,
qualification, legality or completion, stop: do not repair or substitute sources.

Use exactly the existing development fit300/validation100 partition from item20.
No final100, protected66, new cases, trained model, source rewrite, or source
execution. Reuse qualification only for the identical `(problem ID, source hash)`
with a successful receipt in the completed item30 run. A source mismatch blocks;
it does not silently schedule a fresh qualification campaign. Model bytes and
training steps are zero. This is not M2 composition or an authoring exercise.

Four arms start from the identical D11 literal dictionary and identical fixed
source strings: `literal`, `prefix` (L+P), `template` (L+I), and `union` (L+P+I).
A fifth diagnostic `D11` row re-encodes the untouched initial dictionary under
this experiment's canonical policy, without search. The searched literal control
matches search opportunity and additional fitting, rather than attributing all
post-D11 gains to constructors. All arms have one joint dictionary of at most
256 entries, one 8-bit code per chosen entry, and exactly one submit action.

## Typed language and independent decoder

`L` stores a literal; `P` stores the unchanged item5 expression extraction shape;
`I` stores the unchanged item20 identifier-template extraction shape. Their
extractors are reused unchanged. A new typed API unifies their slots, dictionary
ordering, planner and independent Node/Acorn decoder. This is a new grammar and
packet version, not a historical replay of either experiment.

P has 1–3 expression operands, 1–32 fixed ASCII bytes and at most eight nested
P roots. Allowed roots are Binary, Logical, Assignment, Unary, Update,
Parenthesized, Conditional, Member and Call expressions. Each operand is one
literal complete expression or another P. Every root and recursively selected
operand ID is charged. **I is forbidden anywhere inside P.** The decoder checks
complete expressions, AST root/fields, child spans and precedence.

I has 1–4 distinct identifier arguments, 2–96 fixed ASCII bytes and at most 16
recorded uses, with item20's statement/expression root allowlist. Each argument
is a paid literal dictionary entry and is checked as an identifier; I is not
recursive. Repeated uses are represented and charged in the dictionary metadata.
P and I are alternative top-level body edges; they cannot overlap in a packet.
All selected one-character literal IDs count in C, including identifier operands.
`argument_codes` is an attribution subset of the total IDs, not an extra charge
added again to N. Generated bytes separately record P fixed text, I fixed text,
and repeated identifier bytes. An identifier's first use remains its paid arg.

No literal entry may equal any evaluated whole source, solve function, solve
body including braces, or trimmed solve body interior. No P/I expansion may
equal one of those strings. As an additional conservative rule, each constructor
expansion must generate strictly less than half its source length; P counts
its descendants' generated text and I counts fixed plus repeated argument text.
These guards define a new proposed 2c representation, not M1 acceptance.
This changes the admissible language relative to historical5/20 and will be
reported explicitly. No claim about unconstrained prefix/template coding follows.
The independent decoder checks every recorded expansion against the completed
source length, counts recursive generated bytes, and bounds each intermediate
expansion and cumulative program reconstruction at 4MiB; invalid
packets fail the experiment, with no literal fallback or post-validation repair.

The planner minimizes selection count and then the complete lexicographic ID
sequence. P expression planning retains a frontier by generated bytes, selection
count and IDs so the cumulative generated-byte cap cannot silently prune a
needed admissible child. Literal IDs sort by the actually present character
subset followed by other literal text; constructors then sort by typed key.
There is no implicit restoration of all 96 characters. This canonical policy is
used in every arm and diagnostic. No min-C result may be substituted after seeing
validation. Comparison with item24/min-C or flat item30 is not a matched policy
claim; this record compares its own canonical D11 reference and control.

## Fit and selection contract

Mine fit300 only. Constructor support means at least two distinct program IDs:
keep up to 32 P shapes ranked by occurrence-weighted fixed-byte savings and
64 I shapes ranked by occurrence-weighted fixed/repeated-byte savings. The
common literal pool is the union of P spans of length2–64 and I spans of
length2–160, support at least two programs, capped at256 by occurrence savings.
The identical common literal pool is offered to all four arms. All construction
metadata and arbitrary literal content are charged in serialized dictionaries.

Each round offers up to24 contiguous selected-literal merges, up to24 further
common-pool literals and every allowed absent constructor. Probe all removable
entries and retain up to12 lowest-harm deletion candidates for swaps. Infeasible
deletions are counted and remain eligible but can be screened out by this cap.
The initial actual character subset is mandatory, with no additional implicit
characters. Reject any fit coverage loss. Accept strict lower total fit N only;
break candidate ties by full package bytes and ordered keys. Do not accept a
larger fit N to buy C. Search is bounded, not an exact optimum certificate.

Each arm has300 seconds /10000 evaluated dictionaries, including seed and
coverage failures. Four workers receive disjoint pairs of cores0–7,8GiB address
space per process, OMP/BLAS1. AST extraction and pool construction are outside
the inner search clock but inside the outer1800-second process-group supervisor.
No failed phase is restarted under the same freeze. Timeout/incomplete reports
cannot qualify; partial artifacts and process receipts remain available.

Write all four fit dictionaries, plans, pools, traces and hash seals before
loading validation source values for AST extraction/encoding. Source-file hashes
may be checked before fit without parsing source values. Recompute and assert
fit costs against the sealed fit summaries during measurement. Validation cannot
change dictionaries, pools, budgets, source cohort, policy or grammar. Failed
coverage stays in the100-row denominator and forbids full-cohort cost claims.

## Costs and outcomes

Serialize literal dictionaries with LTD1 and typed dictionaries with STD1 plus
canonical JSON `[tag,metadata]` entries. Every literal text, constructor tag,
root kind, field, path, arity, repetition index and fixed string is stored.
Packets contain4-byte magic,1-byte width,32-byte dictionary hash,4-byte count,
then actual8-bit IDs. Thus per-program framing is41 bytes, payload is8N bits,
no padding, actions=N+1. Report full dictionary and all packets as package bytes;
charge the dictionary once per reported cohort, never amortize it away.

Also report package plus decoder dependencies, with the full new mixed decoder
and Acorn charged to every typed arm, including the one-kind ablations. The
literal decoder retains its own dependency charge. This is conservative and not
an optimized specialized-decoder-size comparison. Report offline encoder source
and parser bytes, pinned interpreter/runtime binaries, wall time and per-arm
search trials/time, pool sizes and rejected coverage trials. Complete interpreter
installation bytes are not claimed measured. Offline source mining/optimization
is required; no claim of online task solving or a self-contained small model.

For every arm report fit and validation coverage/qualification, actions,
median/p90/p95/worst, C/N, constructor roots, paid argument IDs, generated bytes,
dictionary/package/decoder-inclusive bytes and paired better/same/worse against
every arm and D11. Common-pool eligibility and each arm's permitted-pool
eligibility are separate from actual use; all per-program rows are retained.
For each reported arm, deterministically select the following six descriptive
subsets from consumed validation, sorted by numeric problem ID:

- `common_pool_eligible`: at least one source AST node matches any P or I in the
  common sealed fit pool, regardless of whether the arm permits that kind.
- `arm_pool_eligible`: at least one node matches a common-pool kind permitted by
  that arm (P for prefix, I for template, both for union, neither for literal/D11).
- `P_used`: the completed packet actually contains at least one paid P root.
- `I_used`: the completed packet actually contains at least one paid I root.
- `either_used`: at least one P or I root occurs in the completed packet.
- `both_used`: at least one of each root kind occurs in the completed packet.

Persist each set's exact IDs/count. On those same IDs report candidate and
searched-literal/untouched-D11 reference total actions, N, C, C/N, packet framing
and payload bytes, and paired better/same/worse plus cost differences. Eligibility
flags are independently reconstructed from AST nodes and the sealed common pool;
used membership is independently checked against the actual packet plan. Eligibility
retains uncovered IDs; a missing packet makes complete subset totals unavailable
instead of omitting its cost. Empty sums are zero, and all zero-denominator ratios
or per-program averages are null.

Only packet bytes are attributable to a subset. Dictionary and decoder overhead
remain global and fully charged: show their full bytes, full dictionary plus
subset packets, and full dictionary plus decoder plus subset packets. Never prune,
amortize or discount dictionary entries for a selected subset. Overlapping subset
packages and counts are not additive. Sets are descriptive, selected using the
consumed source/encoding outcomes, and cannot replace whole-cohort qualification,
change a gate, promote an arm, or support an unconditioned transfer claim.

A structured candidate is ready only if: all400 decode exactly and have matching
successful source qualification; capacity and whole-body audits pass; validation
actions are at least5% below searched literal, median and p90 strictly improve,
at least60/100 improve and at most25 worsen, C/N does not increase, and raw
package bytes are at most105% of literal. It must additionally beat untouched
D11 total actions strictly, with non-worse median/p90/C-fraction, at most25 worse,
and raw package at most105% of D11. These are conservative separate2c gates,
not a relaxation or restatement of flat M1 acceptance. Decoder-inclusive cost is
always reported even though the inherited raw-package gate uses raw package.

The union receives the stronger label `stacking_ready` only if it passes that
rule and also strictly beats both P-only and I-only total actions, with the same
incremental median/p90/breadth/C/package checks. Exact ties are not additive gain.
Report every failed predicate and all pairwise effects even when nothing passes.
Do not choose a simpler arm after validation to call the union a successful stack.
No result updates the flat M1 selected/default dictionary; item24/current flat
successor remains governed by its own record. No universal impossibility claim.

## Falsifiable predictions

These are pre-run guesses, not observations or acceptance requirements. Reporter
fields record the expected truth value (true), observed value and correctness;
failed predictions remain visible and do not select another arm or policy.

1. S1: all2000 program views (400 for each of four searched arms and the
   untouched D11 diagnostic) decode exactly and have matching qualification.
2. S2: prefix has strictly fewer total validation actions than the matched
   searched literal control.
3. S3: template has strictly fewer total validation actions than the matched
   searched literal control.
4. S4: union has strictly fewer total validation actions than both individual
   constructor arms. Ties falsify this prediction.
5. S5: union does **not** pass the complete `stacking_ready` rule, despite the
   predicted action gains, because the joint breadth/quantile/C/package gates
   remain demanding. A passing union falsifies this conservative prediction.

S2–S4 require complete400-view coverage/qualification on both compared arms.
Incomplete supervision yields no evaluated prediction table or success verdict.

## Activation and evidence

`make_contract.py` without arguments creates only `contract.pending.json`.
Later, explicit `--source-run PATH` binds the completed supervised item30 D11
receipt, source/dictionary file hashes and per-problem qualification hashes;
it additionally requires the published independently replayed item30 evidence,
binds its digest to the exact private result digest, and checks that both name
the actual frozen item30 contract digest;
this preparation is not permission to launch. It reads no case files. Root must
verify the frozen item30 contract and independently replayed record, finalize the
new freeze/contract, obtain Claude cross-review and push before `launch.py`.
Use a new private output directory, never overwrite an existing run. Launcher,
runner, reporter and binder reject optimized Python / PYTHONOPTIMIZE so assertion
validation cannot be disabled.

`report.py` replays stored packets and canonical plans, independent typed decode,
source hashes and cost aggregates before writing the separate report. It checks
arm identities, permitted constructor kinds, actual capacity and decoder bytes,
the bound original D11 dictionary/receipts, all fit seals and fit cost totals,
and the final validation seal. Incomplete supervision produces an explicit
no-verdict report retaining its receipt and partial artifacts. It does
not fit, rewrite sources or execute cases. Only aggregate metadata and source
hashes may be committed; source texts, dictionaries, plans, pools and packets
stay in private run storage. Report source provenance and exact receipt hashes,
all coverage/decoder/qualification failures, budget exits and supervisor status.
