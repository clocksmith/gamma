# Fixed-source retest v2: results

Run `fixed-source-v2-r1`, frozen in [FREEZE.md](FREEZE.md) before any v2 dictionary was mined. This file is generated by `make_results.py` from `results/`. Sources, dictionaries, parses and certificates stay private.

**Result.** Dropping the two-file rule changes little at ordinary budgets and a lot only when the dictionary starts storing the corpus. At 256 entries of up to 16 bytes, both rules reach 118,065 tokens. At 4,096 entries of up to 64 bytes, the unrestricted rule uses 75.7% fewer tokens (8,975 against 36,961), but its dictionary stores 202,248 bytes against 53,385 on a 264,908-byte corpus. That is mostly memorization, so token count alone cannot rank these dictionaries.

**Predictions.** 4 of 5 held. Failed: R2.

Everything here is in-sample on 400 paired Python and JavaScript solutions. It says nothing about unseen code, model output or end-to-end compression. No optimum is proved.

## Checks

- 290 chains and 261 polished dictionaries were decoded byte for byte across 26,885 grid cells. 0 chains stopped at a limit.
- The control rebuilt 108 of 108 comparable v1 chains entry for entry.
- 768 shared-against-separate splits were decoded.
- 18 dual certificates were recomputed in exact integers. The certificate is Theorem 1 of [Du and Chen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35869), which we use and do not claim.
- Unrestricted reachability matched its closed form at every tested length.

## Paired v2 against the control and v1

Across 1,440 matched budgets, the unrestricted best is lower in 498, equal in 847 and higher in 95. A larger candidate set cannot be worse at the optimum, so those 95 cells are search failures. Where it is lower, the median reduction is 0.46%, the 90th percentile 11.0% and the maximum 83.4%. Where it is higher, the median increase is 0.10% and the maximum 16.2%.

The large wins sit where the dictionary stores much of the corpus. In the 10 cells storing at least half of it, the reduction is 44.9% to 83.4%. In the other 1,430 cells the median change is 0.00% and the 90th percentile 1.4%.

Against v1, the control's best result is equal in 1,437 cells and lower in 3, where the time-limited local search found a little more.

## Shared against separate

One shared dictionary beat two separate ones in 382 of 384 unrestricted budgets and 384 of 384 control budgets. The direction is guaranteed for optimal dictionaries, because the union of two separate dictionaries fits the same combined budget. Only the size of the gain is empirical. 
The unrestricted exceptions are search failures: 64 bytes, 2,048 entries, 32 KiB (shared 55,547, separate 53,095); 64 bytes, 4,096 entries, 64 KiB (shared 41,250, separate 35,814).

## What this does not show

- Nothing here measures unseen code, a trained model or total encoded size.
- Token counts ignore the cost of storing the dictionary, which is why the largest unrestricted wins are not compression claims.
- All search arms are heuristics, and no cell has a proved optimum.
- The two-file rule counts files, so a string in one problem's Python and JavaScript solutions passes it. A problem-level rule is left to a separately frozen experiment.
