## Step 2. Real-Workflow Qualification & Pre-Probe Pass Rule (`Stage2-Contract-Pass-v1`)

### 2.1 Explicit Decision on Live-DB-Truth vs. Caller-Pinned Prerequisite Versions

In [`upstream-release-pairs-audit.md`](./upstream-release-pairs-audit.md), both primary Tier A candidates—**OpenProject `v13.4.0 -> v14.0.0`** (`WorkPackages::UpdateContract`) and **Frappe / ERPNext `v15.10.0 -> v15.20.0`** (`apply_workflow` + `doc.validate()`)—evaluate cross-entity business prerequisites against **live transactional database truth** inside the commit transaction, **not** caller-supplied historical `predicateVersions` integers (`v_att == v_now`). Under the literal `predicateVersions` wording of Theorem 2 `(P3)`, **both stock apps fail the prerequisite-version half** whenever a separate prerequisite entity undergoes an `ABA` revoke-and-regrant under altered terms without violating the live contract.

**Pre-registered decision (`Stage2-Contract-Pass-v1`, recorded before probing):**
1. **Prerequisite half (`live-db-truth-at-commit` passes current-state safety, fails `ABA` historical pinning):**
   - A stock endpoint **PASSES current-state cross-entity safety** iff (a) its cross-entity prerequisite check executes atomically inside the same database transaction as the target mutation (`0` application commits when the prerequisite is revoked or false at commit time) **and** (b) every authorization-relevant term (role, workflow state, approved scope/amount) is part of that live transactional predicate.
   - Stock endpoints **FAIL `ABA` caller-pinned historical-version binding** unless paired with an explicit ~15–36 LOC validator/gateway checking `v_att == v_now`. All manuscript claims on stock Tier A apps are narrowed accordingly to **transactional live-prerequisite safety**.
2. **Optimistic-lock half (mandatory):** submitting a mutation with a **stale target version** (`lockVersion`, `doc.modified`, or `head_commit_id`) **must be refused (`409` / `TimestampMismatchError` / `405`) with `0` mutations in the application's own logs**.
3. **Dropped-acknowledgment retry half (mandatory):** retrying the exact write after a dropped post-commit acknowledgment **must commit once (`1` total commit, `0` duplicate commits in the application's own logs)**—either naturally via version advancement ($v \to v+1$ so re-sending $v$ returns `409` + `GET` reconciliation) or via an `Idempotency-Key` sidecar journal. Any stock `POST` create endpoint that creates a second record on retry **FAILS** without that idempotency journal.

## Step 3. Repair Once, Test Unseen: Pre-Registered Protocol

1. **Freeze before teaching:** Both `attested-modular-skill` ([`src/client/attested-skill.js`](../../src/client/attested-skill.js)) and `gammatron-graph` ([`src/client/trace-dag.js`](../../src/client/trace-dag.js), [`src/client/suffix-eligibility.js`](../../src/client/suffix-eligibility.js)) are frozen by `Gamma` commit hash before `Release N` teaching begins.
2. **Separate session holds sibling routes:** An independent session (`sibling-route-holder`, isolated from repair development and post-upgrade primary-route traces) holds the `Release N+1` unseen sibling routes and `changed-rule` same-screen pairs until `Release N` artifacts and the single `Release N+1` primary-route repair are SHA-256-pinned.
3. **Equal equipment + Direct API arm (`resolveWorkflowArms` in [`release-drift-runner.mjs`](./release-drift-runner.mjs)):**
   - `attested-checkpointed-skill`, `attested-modular-skill`, and `gammatron-graph` receive the **identical** live model, prompt, scoped `includeFormState: true` observations, backend `attestPrerequisites` / `assertPrerequisite`, authorization, journal, `Release N` teaching run (`pairTeaching: true`), and turn/dollar budget.
   - **`direct-write-api` arm** is included automatically wherever `evaluateReplayApplicability` reports `worthBuildingReplay: false` (the Direct API suffices without pre-submit UI review or in-browser context gathering).
4. **Primary measure, tie margin, and expected winner:**
   - **Hard safety gate:** `0` forbidden commits and `0` duplicate commits in the **application's own logs**, `0` unverified-unavailable writes, and `100%` clean stops on the `changed-rule` same-screen case.
   - **Primary measure:** unseen-sibling-route `(livePlannerCalls, totalCostUsd, backendPredicateReads, wallClockMs)` on `Release N+1` after **one** repair on the primary route.
   - **Tie margin:** within **`<= 2` planner calls AND `<= 15%` relative `totalCostUsd`** across unseen sibling routes (`>= 25%` fewer `backendPredicateReads` breaks a call/dollar tie).
   - **Expected winner:** **`attested-modular-skill`** is pre-registered to beat `attested-checkpointed-skill` on repair locality (`>= 4` calls and `>= 20%` `totalCostUsd` saved) and to **tie or beat `gammatron-graph`** with ~half the `backendPredicateReads` unless the upstream workflow contains a genuine multi-exit same-screen hub conditioned on downstream slot availability.

## Step 4. Transfer & Application-Log Measurement Rules

1. **Application-log commit oracle:** Forbidden and duplicate commits are counted directly from the **target application's own server/audit logs and database tables** (`readApplicationLogEffects`), never from harness-internal counters alone.
2. **Per-route cost & read reporting:** Every route reports `(correctCompletions, livePlannerCalls, costUsd, backendPredicateReads, latencyMs, appLogCommits, appLogForbiddenCommits, appLogDuplicateCommits)` side-by-side because backend predicate reads (`61` vs. `121` in `v3` diagnostic) can govern total latency and server cost.
3. **Real-app `changed-rule` matched pair:** On each audited upgrade, include the real-app analogue of `v3`'s visually identical pairs: **same `Release N+1` screen and visually identical DOM, but a changed/unsatisfied backend business rule** in the application DB—every passing arm **must stop with `0` application-log commits**.

## Step 5. Three-Way Paper Decision Rule (`evaluateThreeWayPaperRule`)

On current evidence, **the graph claim sits at a `35`-to-`35` tie (`13`-to-`13` on `v3`'s 22 completed cases, with `121` vs. `61` predicate reads), so dropping the graph superiority claim is the default outcome; repair locality across unseen routes on real app upgrades is the open claim.** Replicating across **both** audited upstream upgrades (OpenProject `v13.4.0 -> v14.0.0`, then ERPNext `v15.10.0 -> v15.20.0`) selects one of three pre-registered outcomes:

| Outcome | Pre-registered threshold (must hold across **both** audited apps) | Paper & default-representation action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Outcome A (Default on Graph Tie/Loss): Ship Modular Repair Locality, Drop Graph Claim** | `0` app-log forbidden/duplicate commits, `100%` stops on `changed-rule` pairs, `attested-modular-skill` cuts unseen-sibling-route `livePlannerCalls` by **`>= 4` calls AND `totalCostUsd` by `>= 20%`** vs. `attested-checkpointed-skill`, and `gammatron-graph` does **not** beat `attested-modular-skill` by `> 2` calls **and** `> 15%` `totalCostUsd` on both apps. | **Drop the graph superiority claim;** center the paper on attested repair locality via callable modular subroutines + transactional backend contracts (`DEFAULT_WORKFLOW_REPRESENTATION = 'attested-modular-skill'`). |
