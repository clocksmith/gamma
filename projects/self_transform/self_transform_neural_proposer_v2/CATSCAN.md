# CATSCAN: Multi-seed cumulative neural proposer heredity study (v2)

Parent: [Self-transform studies](../CATSCAN.md)

## Target

Demonstrate repeatable, multi-generational cold-target self-improvement across `S = 5` independent lineage seeds (`G = 4` generations) using a split-half-coherence SwiGLU neural proposer (`M_Phi_v2`), beating both fixed `Gen-0` and equal-budget conventional controls (`SGD+Nesterov`, `AdamW`, `Damped L-BFGS`) on an untouched four-family unseen neural confirmation set.

## Authority

- Owns the `v1` plateau investigation, `v2` neural proposer, multi-seed subprocess lineage runner, candidate ledger, tests, and receipts under `projects/self_transform/self_transform_neural_proposer_v2/`.
- Preserves prior `projects/self_transform_*` artifacts unchanged.

## Scope

- Five independent evolutionary seeds, four generations across `exact`, `regen` (4-generation weight-free behavioral regeneration), and `fixed_proposer` (`M_{Phi_0}`) lineages (`1,320 * g` target passes + `80 * g` reconstruction allowance matched against `1,400 * g` conventional calibration), complete candidate ledger, and `64` untouched unseen confirmation tasks.

## Contracts

- Input: [Gamma research projects charter](../../CATSCAN.md), `v1` diagnostic evidence, and frozen protocol locks written before confirmation.
- Output: Complete candidate ledger JSON, per-generation/per-seed held-out curves, paired statistical tests, and reproduction scripts.

## Invariants

- Every cold-target evaluation starts with `state = None` and consumes an identical `B = 10` forward-backward pass budget.
- Conventional calibration receives `1,400 * g` passes (`g = 1..4`), strictly covering `M_Phi_v2`'s `1,320 * g` selection/sparring passes plus `80 * g` behavioral reconstruction FLOP allowance.
- The final confirmation population (`seed_base = 850000`) remains untouched until all `v2` rules freeze.

## Acceptance

- [Unit tests](test_proposer_v2.py) verify budget equality, split-half coherence zero-pass overhead, elitist selection, and subprocess isolation.

## Non-goals

- Claiming source-code quines or open-ended LLM self-modification beyond measured neural target families.

## Freedom

- Any implementation preserving these contracts, matched budgets, and untouched confirmation splits.
