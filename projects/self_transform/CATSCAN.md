# CATSCAN: Self-transform studies

Parent: [Gamma research projects](../CATSCAN.md)

## Target

Keep versioned self-transform studies inspectable and reproducible without
collapsing distinct operators, evaluators or scientific evidence.

## Authority

Owns family navigation and local test entrypoints; each original study owns its
mechanism, protocol, source closure and interpretation.

## Invariants

- Original directory names, cross-version imports and frozen source bytes remain.
- Reconstruction and oracle checks do not establish autonomous improvement.
- No GPU, network, model download or new study is part of the test frontdoor.
- Retained receipts preserve their original paths and source revisions.

## Acceptance

The [family index](README.md) lists the Node and Python CPU checks.

## Non-goals

A single replacement mechanism, a Python distribution, resumed research, or a
claim that any study establishes general model improvement.
