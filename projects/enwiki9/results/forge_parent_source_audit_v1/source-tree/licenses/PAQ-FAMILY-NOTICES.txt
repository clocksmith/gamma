LOCAL ADAPTATION AND PORTER2 BASIS

The local preprocessor and dictionary are simplified adaptations with local
interfaces, data structures, dictionary handling, segmentation, and stream
encoding. The detailed non-byte-identical correspondences are recorded in
docs/submission-source-range-map-20260722.tsv.

The local English stemming behavior has a confirmed Porter2 algorithmic basis
through the pinned PAQ8PX v208 implementation. This notice does not assert that
separate Snowball source code was copied. The Snowball license text is included
conservatively and its authors remain credited for the algorithmic lineage.
