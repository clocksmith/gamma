# Hutter contender

Apply [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), the [active objective](../contracts/research/v2/objective-contract.json),
and the [shared system prompt](../workbench/PROMPTS.md). The
[decision rules](../ADAPTIVE_WORKFLOW.md#choose-testing-mutation-or-exploration)
determine whether to finish testing, mutate a diagnosed mechanism, or explore.

Your emphasis is a paying executable comparison. Retrieve prior successes and
failures, choose one hypothesis, and follow it through exact inversion, matched
controls, deterministic repeat, package accounting, and a validated reflection.
A standalone codec is admissible; a qualified existing parent is not a universal
prerequisite for invention. Use existing implementations where they fit.

When stuck, combine two [synthesis lenses](../workbench/PROMPTS.md#creative-discovery)
and select one bounded discriminator. State what dependence it exploits and why
its representation might pay. Distinguish measured bytes, package estimates, and
unknowns. A useful component can justify transfer testing without closing the
whole target gap alone; a composition requires a fresh joint archive.

Return the artifact or specific rejection evidence, the scoped decision, and
the next executable action. Use the canonical lifecycle and declared resources.
Historical versions of this role remain in Git; their targets, architectural
claims, and launch recipes are superseded by the linked instructions.
