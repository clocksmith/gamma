## Frozen frame language

Parse the WRT stream into complete emission groups. A lexical anchor is exactly
five consecutive emission groups whose decoded surface:

- contains at least two alphabetic word groups of length at least two;
- contains only ASCII letters, spaces, comma, period, apostrophe, hyphen, and
  parentheses; and
- lies completely inside one page text element.

A candidate rule has exactly two anchors and one literal hole:

```text
GEN_FRAME(left_anchor, LITERAL(hole), right_anchor)
```

The hole contains one through twelve complete emission groups. Rule identity
is the exact pair of WRT anchor byte strings. A development rule is legal only
when it occurs on at least two distinct development pages and has at least two
distinct exact WRT hole realizations. Rule discovery, anchor width, hole range,
surface alphabet, and page threshold are frozen before selection and sealed
confirmation are measured.

For each population, exact weighted interval scheduling selects a
nonoverlapping set of complete frame envelopes that maximizes displaced parent
integer qbits. Equal-score choices exclude the later candidate, giving a
canonical result.

## Exact zero-cost accounting

For selected frames, the two anchor byte intervals do not consume arithmetic
truth bits. Literal holes and all unselected bytes retain their original
receipt-bound P1 rows. Construct and terminate the actual residual range
payload; do not estimate the gain by qbits alone.

The decoder receives frame rules and invocations out of band only for this
zero-cost certificate. It must decode literal bits, insert exact anchors,
reconstruct the complete WRT stream, and reproduce the raw input through the
official inverse. This is deliberately optimistic: any finite paid grammar is
at least as large.

## Controls

```text
E0  exact parent final-P1 replay
L1  zero-hole contiguous ten-group lexical phrases frozen on two development pages
L2  one-slot cross-page lexical frames; the proposed mechanism
```

L1 measures ordinary repeated lexical phrases under the same zero-cost bypass.
LR preserves the learned left/right anchor inventories but breaks their paired
frame identity before exact corpus matching. Every control uses actual
terminated arithmetic payloads and exact WRT reconstruction.
