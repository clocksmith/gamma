# MÖBIUS-2 LOGOS semantic-role frame ceiling Q0

Candidate: `mobius2_logos_semantic_role_frame_ceiling_q0_v1`

Status: frozen zero-credit opening-1M information ceiling.

## Question

The ordered-template LOGOS ceiling saved 291 B/M and the one-hole lexical
frame ceiling saved 1,064 B/M before command costs. Both exact surface event
universes are retired. This experiment asks a materially broader question:

> Can sentence-level semantic-role patterns group different lexical
> realizations and generate enough exact WRT function, relation, punctuation,
> and shell bytes to clear 3,000 B/M before rules or invocations are charged?

This is not another anchor-width or hole-width sweep. A frame may contain many
variable-length typed slots, and its fixed words are compared through frozen
semantic classes such as copula, birth, location, creation, naming,
membership, quantity, and temporal relation.

## Frozen sentence population

Only complete page `<text>` regions are eligible. Clauses end at `.`, `!`,
`?`, `;`, or newline. Retain clauses with 6 through 128 complete WRT emission
groups and 24 through 512 decoded raw bytes. Exclude template, table, ref,
gallery, category, file, image, and raw XML-tag clauses. Require at least 12
ASCII letters, two typed slots, three classified word atoms, and one relation
atom.

Page titles and Wiki link targets are marked from exact completed raw syntax.
Every WRT emission group is classified as one of:

```text
TITLE
LINK
ENTITY
DATE
NUMBER
QUANTITY
URL
CONTENT
```

Unknown alphabetic words become literal slots. Adjacent equal-class slots,
including intervening whitespace, collapse into one variable-length slot.

The compiled word ontology is frozen in the gate source. It contains exact
closed classes for determiners, copulas, auxiliaries, possession, conjunction,
relative clauses, location/time/general prepositions, quantifiers, negation,
birth, death, creation, service, location, naming, inclusion, type,
membership, measurement, and change. Punctuation and whitespace use frozen
surface classes. Unknown bytes remain exact surface atoms.

## Rule definitions

For each eligible clause construct three keys.

```text
S1 surface frame:
    exact fixed WRT groups + typed slot sequence

S2 semantic-role frame:
    canonical word/punctuation classes + typed slot sequence

SR rotated semantic control:
    S2 with every slot class deterministically rotated by page, clause,
    and slot index
```

The actual WRT realization of every generated group is retained in the
out-of-band Q0 plan. S2 therefore permits multiple lexical realizations of one
canonical frame. It is deliberately an optimistic ceiling: rule definitions,
realization choices, slot lengths, invocation positions, framing, and source
cost zero bytes.

A development rule requires:

- occurrences on at least two distinct development pages;
- at least two distinct complete slot-surface tuples;
- for S2 and SR, at least two distinct fixed-surface realizations.

Selection and sealed pages cannot create rules. They may only instantiate
development-frozen keys.

## Exact residual construction

For every qualifying occurrence, omit only its fixed WRT emission groups from
an actual terminated range payload. All slot bits remain literal with their
receipt-bound endpoint428 probabilities. The out-of-band rule plan restores
the generated groups before the official WRT inverse. Every generated bit is
understood to traverse the unchanged Gamma state update path; native state
hashing is a later obligation and this oracle receives zero score credit.

Controls:

```text
B0  exact complete parent replay
S1  exact surface multi-slot frames
S2  semantic-role and lexical-realization frames
SR  deterministically rotated slot-role labels
SL  forced-literal parent payload
```

Every control constructs and terminates its payload, decodes its literal bits,
reconstructs exact WRT bytes, and produces a byte-identical second payload.
S2 additionally passes the official raw inverse.

## Promotion and kill rule

Authorize one paid semantic grammar Q1 only if all conditions pass:

```text
S2 exact gross gain                         >= 3,000 B/M
development-only S2 contribution           > 0
selection-only S2 contribution             > 0
sealed-only S2 contribution                > 0
S2 payload                                 < S1 payload
S2 payload                                 < SR payload
parent payload identity                     exact
all residual arithmetic decodes             exact
all WRT reconstructions                     exact
official raw inverse                        exact
all second payloads                         byte-identical
all input hashes                            exact
```

On a miss, retire this exact ontology, sentence boundary, slot system,
realization abstraction, and rule-admission contract. Do not sweep word
classes, sentence widths, minimum pages, slot counts, or punctuation classes.
A valid rejection exits zero. Identity, replay, inverse, or malformed-input
failure exits nonzero.

No distant, native, 10M, 100M, or full-1G run is authorized by Q0 alone. The
forecast remains 109,389,323 bytes and full-1G remains unknown until a counted
constructive codec exists.
