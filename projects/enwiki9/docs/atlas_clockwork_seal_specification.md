# Private Atlas-Clockwork Application Seal

Classification: `ORGANIZER ONLY`
Seal version: `ACS-MATH-SEAL-1`

## 1. Purpose

This document is not part of the mathematical examination and must not be sent
to solvers. It defines the private reduction from each independent theorem
problem to a hidden finite application.

The public solver supplies only a mathematical proof and symbolic construction.
The organizer is responsible for:

- Proving that the hidden application satisfies the theorem's hypotheses.
- Applying the proved construction.
- Instantiating every finite parameter.
- Implementing and validating the resulting finite representation.
- Counting every required bit and resource.
- Establishing exact transfer to the bound application.

A public proof is not declared transferable unless the corresponding private
hypothesis and application certificates pass.

## 2. Frozen private commitments

Before publishing the examination, freeze and hash:

1. The public problem-set version.
2. The hidden input identity.
3. The hidden baseline or teacher identity.
4. One hypothesis certificate for each route.
5. One application map for each route.
6. Exact accounting rules.
7. Exact reversibility rules.
8. Exact resource rules.
9. The application verifier.
10. The final transfer-verification procedure.

Publication may expose only the commitment hash and timestamp. The contents
remain private until disclosure is appropriate.

## 3. Route A private reduction

### 3.1 Interpretation

Map the hidden finite application into the gain matrix of Problem A:

- Rows are paid-information blocks.
- Columns are candidate finite explanations or prompts.
- Entry \(g_{jk}\) is the exact jointly replayed gain before advice cost.
- Prefix length \(\ell_k\) is the exact paid selector cost.
- Description price \(d_k\) is the exact surviving representation cost.

### 3.2 Hypothesis certificate

The organizer must prove that the hidden gain matrix is generated by legal
causal predictions and that every paid explanation can be transmitted before
first use.

### 3.3 Application criterion

After applying the solver's variational theorem and construction, instantiate
the selected explanations, prefix code, and block assignments. Perform one
exact combined replay. The route transfers only if the complete resulting
artifact meets the hidden target and every execution requirement.

No gain-matrix optimum receives credit without exact joint replay.

## 4. Route B private reduction

### 4.1 Interpretation

Map the hidden causal context system to Problem B:

- Finite hidden histories become \(H\).
- Decoder-visible events become alphabet \(A\).
- Predictive signatures become colors \(C\).
- Causal updates become transition \(T\).
- Exact continuation geometry induces the labeled quotient graph.

### 4.2 Hypothesis certificate

Prove that the hidden transition is total on the declared finite domain, every
color is causally available or finitely represented, and the resulting graph
satisfies every hypothesis used by the solver's theorem.

### 4.3 Application criterion

Apply the solver's behavioral quotient, distinguishing certificates, Wheeler
order or canonical refinement, and continuation bound. Instantiate a finite
causal predictor from the resulting classes and intervals. Transfer requires
exact combined coding, counted representation, exact inversion, and target and
resource success.

## 5. Route C private reduction

### 5.1 Interpretation

Map the hidden teacher recurrence to Problem C:

- Selective symbols become \(\mathcal A\).
- Teacher transitions become \(A_a,b_a\).
- Teacher output becomes \(c,\gamma_a\).
- The fixed norm and contraction factor become the certified stability data.

### 5.2 Hypothesis certificate

Prove exact rational or conservatively bounded values for:

- Contraction factor \(\rho<1\).
- State norm.
- Matrix and bias magnitudes.
- Output-vector norm.
- Sequence length.
- Available cumulative loss allowance.

If the actual teacher is not contractive under the declared norm, Route C is
not bound and no public solution may be claimed transferable through it.

### 5.3 Application criterion

Apply the solver's integer-shadowing, Householder realization, and precision
threshold. Construct the finite integer recurrence and perform exact joint
coding. Transfer requires the exact total loss, representation growth,
resource use, inversion, and final target to pass.

### 6.3 Application criterion

## 7. Independence

Each route has a distinct private hypothesis certificate and application map.
A route may not use another route's theorem, proof, parameters, measured gain,
or implementation.

The private acceptance condition is

\[
\operatorname{TRANSFER}_A
\lor
\operatorname{TRANSFER}_B
\lor
\operatorname{TRANSFER}_C
\lor
\operatorname{TRANSFER}_D.
\]

A public proof may be mathematically correct while its private route fails
because hidden hypotheses or target margins do not hold. Such a proof remains a
valid examination solution but is not represented as a successful transfer.

## 8. Exact application verification

For any route claimed transferable, the organizer must produce:

- Bound input hash.
- Complete transformed or predicted representation.
- Complete package and model accounting.
- Exact encode/decode roundtrip.
- Deterministic second artifact.
- Exact joint arithmetic or reconstruction receipt.
- Peak-memory receipt.
- Encode and decode timing receipts.
- Source-to-theorem parameter mapping.
- Theorem-to-artifact construction mapping.
- Final target decision.

Forecasts, proxy loss, oracle gains, asymptotic savings, and separately added
components do not establish transfer.

## 9. Private transfer theorem

For route \(i\), let:

- \(S_i\) be a complete correct public mathematical solution.
- \(H_i\) be the frozen private proof that the bound application satisfies its
  hypotheses.
- \(\Psi_i\) be the frozen application construction.
- \(V_i\) be the exact private verifier.

Transfer is established only by a machine-checked and mathematically audited
receipt proving

\[
S_i\land H_i
\Longrightarrow
V_i(\Psi_i(S_i))=\operatorname{PASS}.
\]

Without \(H_i\), \(\Psi_i\), and a passing \(V_i\) receipt, the organizer must
not claim that solving the public theorem wins or qualifies for any external
application.
