## Purpose

Problems A through D have complete mathematical solutions, subject to the
recorded C1, D3, and D4 corrections. This memorandum identifies exactly what
those solutions provide, what they do not provide, and which organizer-owned
construction would be required for a target-bearing transfer.

The target remains an exact counted score at or below `108,000,000` bytes on
the exact `1,000,000,000`-byte corpus, with deterministic roundtrip and
eligible CPU runtime and memory. The current planning value is a forecast, not
an exact full-corpus baseline.

## Problem A: paid information

### Mathematically available

- Exact finite duality between paid explanations and empirical assignment
  frequencies.
- Canonical optimal relaxed weights.
- A constructive integer prefix code within less than one bit per block of the
  relaxed value.
- Exact description-price accounting.
- Sharp perturbation stability.

### Canonical extraction

Given a frozen legal gain matrix and explanation descriptions, choose the
lexicographically first optimal assignment, then the lexicographically first
minimum-price legal prefix code.

### Missing antecedent

No explanation family currently supplies enough independent exact gain. On
opening 1M, page calibration achieved only `+7` gross oracle bytes and lost
after labels. Nine existing endpoint traces achieved only `+6` gross oracle
bytes and also lost after labels.

### Reopening condition

Supply materially new causal explanations or a different selection granularity
whose exact gross oracle gain clears a predeclared target-scale threshold before
selector construction. Existing endpoint subsets and label tuning are excluded.

## Problem B: predictive Wheeler quotient

### Mathematically available

- The unique coarsest behavior-preserving right congruence.
- Finite distinguishing words and minimality certificates.
- Wheeler interval geometry for every continuation.
- A finite canonical unfolding with exact size bounds.

### Canonical extraction

Given a finite causal predictor state system and exact predictive colors,
construct its behavioral quotient and lexicographically first valid Wheeler
serialization or the committed finite unfolding.

### Missing antecedent

The theorem supplies index geometry, not predictive information. No finite
coloring has demonstrated target-scale residual gain against the mature
endpoint, and no complete index, package, runtime, or memory accounting exists.

### Reopening condition

An offline exact-continuation oracle must first show substantial gain on
disjoint distant windows using decoder-visible equivalence signatures. Only
then may an index be implemented.

## Problem C: integer recurrence shadowing

### Mathematically available

- Uniform shadowing of contractive selective recurrences.
- A rigorous upper bound on cumulative excess loss under dyadic approximation.
- Rational Householder realization and perturbation bounds.
- An explicit sufficient fixed-point precision.

### Canonical extraction

Given a frozen contractive teacher, a frozen supplied factorization, and
certified constants, choose the least dyadic precision satisfying the public
sufficient bound, then instantiate the committed factor order, rounding rule,
and deterministic integer recurrence.

### Missing antecedent

There is no locally certified, exact, full-corpus under-target teacher with
global contraction, sufficient score slack, and official resource receipts.
The public theorem also assumes rather than discovers a useful matrix
factorization. Without a teacher and a frozen supplied factorization, it cannot
guarantee an under-target student.

### Reopening condition

Reproduce an independently under-target teacher on exact enwik9, freeze its
probability and package accounting, prove or enforce contraction, and show that
the C1-C4 loss allowance plus implementation growth fits inside measured score
slack.

## Problem D: energy-ordered parity reconstruction

### Mathematically available

- Exact kernel-collision characterization.
- Finite separating maps and canonical nested extension.
- Difference-set and Hamming-distance uniqueness criteria.
- Exact first-hit verification for a bounded ordered candidate list.

### Canonical extraction

Given a frozen energy, residual set, and bounded candidate enumerator, choose
the lexicographically first minimum-depth separating matrix and emit the
canonical first-hit certificate.

### Missing antecedent

The theorem does not create a low-rank true residual or an efficient search.
General WRT MOIRAI evidence is negative. No search-decomposable energy,
target-bearing syndrome archive, or eligible expansion bound exists.

### Reopening condition

Find a constrained structural region with a deterministic prototype and sparse
residuals. Exact enumeration at microblock scale must show low true rank;
bounded parity-aware search must approach direct-rank length and beat causal
coding after framing before block size increases.

## Combined conclusion

The public mathematics is complete enough for its current purposes. The next
work is not another general theorem set. It is one of:

1. Produce a new, high-leverage legal explanation family for Route A.
2. Demonstrate exact residual gain before constructing a Route B index.
3. Reproduce and certify an under-target teacher for Route C.
4. Demonstrate low-rank, efficiently searchable structural residuals for
   Route D.

The routes remain independent under Seal-2. A future composed compressor may
use several solved theorems, but that composition would require a new private
application seal and exact joint replay. It cannot inherit additive score
credit from the independent public routes.

No candidate distribution is authorized while the Seal remains `UNBOUND`.
